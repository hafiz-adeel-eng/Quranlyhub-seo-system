FEES (in euros)
- 2 classes a week: 30 euros per month.
- 4 classes a week: 50 euros per month.
- If the student asks for a lower price for 4 classes a week, the teacher can agree to 40 euros per month.
  First say the normal price (50 euros). Offer 40 euros ONLY if the student asks for a discount.

CLASS DAYS
- Best days for the teacher: Saturday and Sunday. (The student has also said these days are good.)
- If the student wants to change: Monday can be used instead of Saturday (Sunday and Monday).
- If the student wants more days in the week: any day is possible, with the class time after 2 PM teacher's time.

NOORANI QAIDA - LESSON ORDER (the page numbers depend on the book; follow what the teacher says)
- Single Arabic letters (Alif, Baa, Taa, Thaa, Jeem ...), one by one.
- Joined letters: how letters change shape when they join.
- Harakat: fathah, kasrah, dammah.
- Tanween.
- Standing harakat (khari fathah, khari kasrah, ulti dammah).
- Madd letters and leen letters.
- Sukoon (jazm).
- Shaddah (tashdeed).
- Then reading short words and verses, and moving to the Quran.

TEACHING STYLE
- Teach a little at a time. Praise the student often ("Very good!", "Excellent!", "Well done!").
- The teacher reads first, then the student repeats.
- At the end of the class, give a short summary and tell the student what we will learn next time.
